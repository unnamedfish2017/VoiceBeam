# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — 2026-10-24

First public release.

- **One file.** `VoiceBeam.bat` is a short cmd launcher plus PowerShell, which Windows 10 and 11 already have. Double-click it; nothing to install, on the PC or on the phone.
- **Phone to cursor.** Dictate with the phone's own voice keyboard, tap Send, and the text is pasted at the cursor of the window in front (clipboard + Ctrl+V, Enter optional, the previous text clipboard is put back).
- **Two styles.** *Beads* (拼豆) — cream paper, bold outlines, a strip of bead tiles — and *Business*, each in light and dark; English and 简体中文.
- **A big text box** that fills the screen and stays usable with the keyboard up (18px type, 22/26 px in settings).
- **After Send** the keyboard stays up but the phone's dictation stops; "Close the keyboard" is one tap away in settings.
- **A QR code that fits:** drawn two modules per line where the console allows it, measured rather than guessed.
- **Guarded:** other websites cannot use your browser to type on the PC (Host, Origin and Content-Type checks); nothing leaves your network.
